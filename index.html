Getting started

  • Open the Schedule tab to see what's airing today and across the week.
  • Tap + on any poster to follow that show.
  • Followed shows appear in My List, grouped by the day their next episode lands.
  • Discover lets you search, browse next season, and see what's returning from hiatus.

Common questions

Why is My List empty when I first open the app?

That's expected. Animiru doesn't guess what you watch — go to Schedule or Discover and follow a few shows, and they'll appear in My List.

The air time looks wrong

Animiru converts Japanese broadcast times to your device's local time zone. A show listed as late-night Tuesday in Japan can legitimately land on Monday or Tuesday morning where you are. If a time still looks wrong, email me the show name and your time zone.

A show is missing

Show data comes from AniList, a community-maintained database. If an entry is missing or wrong there, it will be missing or wrong in Animiru. Corrections made on AniList flow through on the next sync.

Nothing is loading

Animiru needs a connection for its first sync. If it can't reach the network you'll see a banner saying so, and it will keep showing whatever it last downloaded. Pull down on My List to retry.

The "Open in…" buttons don't always land on the right episode

This one is a genuine limitation, so it's worth being straight about. Animiru can ask another app to open a title, but it can't control what that app then does — some providers accept a link and still drop you on their home screen. That's why the buttons say Open rather than Watch. Availability also varies by region, and the catalog data is global.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your watchlist lives on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Animiru stores is local, so it goes with it. There's no server-side account to close — see the privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Animiru schedules local reminders for episodes of shows you follow. These are generated on your device; nothing about your watchlist is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications → Animiru.
