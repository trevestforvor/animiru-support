Privacy Policy for Animiru

Last updated: August 11, 2026

The short version

Animiru does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your watchlist stays on your device.

What Animiru stores, and where

Animiru stores the following only on your device:

  • The list of shows you follow
  • Cached show information (titles, cover art, air times, synopses)
  • A small backup copy of your watchlist, so it survives if the system reclaims storage space
  • On Apple TV, a short list of shows airing today, so the Top Shelf row can display them

Animiru does not transmit any of this. There is no Animiru server to send it to, and we could not read your watchlist even if we wanted to. If you delete the app, this data goes with it.

One honest caveat about iCloud. Animiru does not use iCloud itself — it has no iCloud entitlement, no CloudKit database, and no iCloud sync. But like any app's local data, what Animiru stores on your device can be included in your own iCloud Backup if you have device backups turned on in iOS or tvOS settings. That backup belongs to you and Apple, is encrypted, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy — the developer of Animiru has no access to it and receives nothing from it. You can control this under Settings → your name → iCloud. We mention it because "stays on your device" would otherwise be slightly too neat: your data may live in your own backup as well.

What leaves your device

Animiru fetches anime information from one public, community-maintained database:

  • AniList — https://graphql.anilist.co

These requests are anonymous and read-only. They ask questions like "what anime is airing this season?" They do not include your name, email, device identifier, advertising identifier, location, or your watchlist. We do not send AniList any information about you, because the app does not have any.

As with any internet request, AniList receives your device's IP address at the network level, as is technically unavoidable for any app that connects to the internet. Their handling of that is governed by their own policy:

  • AniList: https://anilist.co/terms

Animiru also loads cover images from the image URLs AniList provides.

What Animiru does NOT do

  • No user accounts or authentication
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • No tracking across apps or websites (no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to ask permission for)
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data to sell or share
  • No location access
  • No contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Animiru schedules episode reminders locally on your device for the shows you follow. These are local notifications. No push server is involved and nothing about your watchlist is transmitted to send them. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Children's privacy

Animiru collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from any user because we do not collect data at all.

Third-party content

Anime titles, synopses, air times, and cover artwork are provided by AniList and remain the property of their respective rights holders. Animiru is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by AniList or any streaming service.

Your rights

Because Animiru holds no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on request. To remove all data Animiru has stored locally, delete the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
